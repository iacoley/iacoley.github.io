Postdoc Colloquium

Rutgers · Department of Mathematics
Hill Center – Busch Campus
110 Frelinghuysen Road

This colloquium meets 5 - 6:20p Wednesdays in Hill 705. Its purpose is to foster social interaction between graduate students and postdocs through short talks of general mathematical interest.
